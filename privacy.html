Hoops Offline – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Hoops Offline ("the app") is an offline 3D basketball game for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles.

No accounts, no personal data collected by us

The app has no sign-up, login or user accounts, and it does not run its own servers. We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Data stored only on your device

The app saves a few game settings and progress values (for example your chosen time of day, number of games played and saved game state) in local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, including full-screen ads during timeouts and optional rewarded ads. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions with ads (for example ad views and taps)
  • Diagnostic and performance information (for example crash logs)

This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
