FAQ

Does the game need internet? No. Everything plays offline. A connection is only used to load ads.

How do I shoot? Hold SHOOT and let go when the meter reaches the green zone. Further shots and tighter defense make the green zone smaller.

How do I dunk? Sprint (hold RUN) into the paint with room from your defender, then release SHOOT at the top of the jump.

Who gets my pass? The teammate with the blue ring under him. Aim the joystick to change who it is.

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.
